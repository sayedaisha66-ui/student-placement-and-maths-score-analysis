Student Placement and Mathematics Performance Analysis
This project explores a dataset of 105 students, comparing their mathematics performance and GPA with recorded placement outcomes: fail, inland, and foreign.
I used MySQL to query and summarize the data, then built a Power BI report to visualize the results. The analysis includes average math scores by placement group, subject-level comparisons, and patterns across student groups.
The findings describe associations in this dataset; they do not show why a student received a placement outcome or establish cause and effect. The dataset does not include a direct measure of students’ interest in mathematics.
Files: SQL queries, dashboard screenshots.
Business Questions that were answered in the dashboard are as follows
1- What is the overall placement distribution among Inland, Foreign, and Failed students?
2-What is the overall placement rate of students?
3-What is the average GPA of the student population?
4-What is the average mathematics score across all students?
5-How does GPA vary across placement outcomes?
6-How does average mathematics performance differ between placed and unplaced students?
7-Which placement category demonstrates the strongest academic performance?
8-How do mathematics subject scores vary by placement status?
9-Which mathematics subjects are most closely associated with successful placements?
10-Do students with lower mathematics scores have a higher likelihood of remaining unplaced?
11-How does placement distribution differ by gender?
12-What is the relationship between family wealth and placement outcomes?
13-What academic characteristics distinguish successfully placed students?
14-What factors differentiate inland-placed, foreign-placed, and unplaced students?
